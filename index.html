1. Overview

Doc Unlock ("the app") is an Android app published by Vumbiwa The Creation PTY LTD ("we", "us"). It helps you remove restrictions and known passwords from PDF and Word documents.

In short: your documents are processed on your phone and are never uploaded to us. We don't require an account. The app shows ads and uses basic analytics provided by Google, which are described below.

2. Your documents

  • Files you choose are opened and processed entirely on your device.
  • We do not upload, copy, read remotely, or store your documents on any server.
  • The app makes temporary working copies in its private storage while it works. These are not accessible to other apps.
  • Unlocked files are saved only where you choose, or shared only with the app you pick.

3. Passwords

If you enter a document password so the app can remove it, the password is used once, in your device's memory, to open that file. It is never stored, logged, or transmitted.

4. Information collected

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, or document contents. However, the Google services built into the app automatically collect limited technical information:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) may collect your Advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system details, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • Firebase Analytics may collect an app instance ID, device and app version details, and general app usage and diagnostic events.
  • Firebase Remote Config fetches app settings (such as how often ads are shown) and may use an app instance ID to do so.

This information is handled by Google under its own privacy policies. We use it only to operate, measure, and improve the app and to show ads that keep it free.

5. Data stored on your device

  • Unlock history: a simple local log of file names, results, and times, shown on the History screen. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it at any time from the History screen.
  • Temporary files: working copies are removed when you clear the list or close the app, and in any case when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

6. Permissions

Doc Unlock does not request broad storage access. It uses Android's built-in file picker and folder picker, so the app can only reach the specific files and folders you select. It uses the internet only for ads, analytics, and remote settings, and uses the network-state permission so ads can load efficiently.

7. Third-party services

The app uses the following services, which have their own privacy practices:

  • Google AdMob and Google Play services: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data in ads
  • Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

8. Your choices

  • Ads personalization: on your Android device, open Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path varies by device) to reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization.
  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice in the app.
  • Clear your data: clear the History screen or the app's storage in Android settings at any time.

9. Children's privacy

Doc Unlock is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help resolve it.

10. Retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you on our own servers. Data on your device is removed when you clear it or uninstall the app. Data held by Google's services is retained under Google's policies linked above.

11. Security

Because your documents never leave your device, they are protected by your device's own security. We recommend keeping your phone's operating system up to date and using a screen lock.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last revised, and continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
